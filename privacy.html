Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026

What happens on your TV stays on your TV. Umbryx TV has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing about what you watch is ever sent to me.

What Umbryx TV keeps on your TV

To work, Umbryx TV saves these things on the TV itself:

  • Your profiles: names, icons, colours, themes and app layouts. PINs are stored scrambled, not as the numbers you typed.
  • Kids profile settings: the apps they're allowed, their daily time limit and bedtime.
  • Screen time: how long each profile used each app, for the weekly report. It's kept for about five weeks, then deleted.
  • Photos you add for the screensaver or the "Your Photo" theme.
  • Your guest Wi-Fi name and password, if you set up the guest Wi-Fi tile.
  • Calendar links you add, and your town for the weather.
  • Game night scores and any quiz questions you write.
  • Phone remote sign-ins: the name each person types and the type of phone they're using.

All of this stays on the TV. Uninstalling Umbryx TV deletes it.

What leaves your TV

Only three things, and only to do the job they're for:

  • Weather. Your town's name and map position go to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, to get the forecast.
  • Calendars. If you link a calendar (Google, Apple or another), the TV downloads it from that link.
  • Updates. Once a day the TV checks GitHub for a new version of Umbryx TV and downloads it if there is one. GitHub sees your internet address, as any website does.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. I can't see your TV, your screen time or your settings after I leave.

The phone remote

The phone remote runs on the TV itself, not on the internet. It only works for phones on your home Wi-Fi. The owner code unlocks the settings; friends who scan the code on the TV get a guest remote that can't change settings or install anything.

Network debugging

For the phone remote to press buttons and type, Umbryx TV uses an Android setting called Network debugging. With it on, a device on your home Wi-Fi could ask to connect to the TV, but the TV asks you first, and only devices you allow can connect. Keep your Wi-Fi password to people you trust. You can turn Network debugging off at any time; the phone remote's buttons then stop working, and everything else keeps working.

What I keep about customers

When you book an installation or contact me, I keep your name, phone number, email, address, TV model and what you bought. I use these to install Umbryx TV, help you later and keep my business records. I don't share or sell them. Ask me any time to see or delete them (I may need to keep payment records for tax).

While I'm installing, I see your TV's screen to set things up. I don't copy anything from it.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, tracking or analytics. It's hosted by GitHub and uses a font from Google, so they may log your visit the way any website host does. If you pick a theme on the home page, your browser remembers it on your own device.
