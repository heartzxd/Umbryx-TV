Questions.

Which TVs does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV: most smart TVs from brands like Sony, TCL, Hisense and Philips, and streaming boxes like the Chromecast with Google TV.

Will my apps still work?

Yes. Umbryx TV is the home screen. Netflix, YouTube, Disney+ and the rest open exactly as they do now.

Can I go back to the normal home screen?

Any time. Hold the Home button on your remote for the original Google TV home.

Do I need to set anything up myself?

No. I install it at your home and set everything up for you, including the phone remote.

Who can use the phone remote?

Only phones on your home Wi-Fi. Friends who scan the code get a guest remote, and your owner code unlocks everything else.
