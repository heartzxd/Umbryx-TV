How to check your TV

  1. Look at the home screen. If it says "Google TV" or has rows of apps with a Google search bar or microphone, it's almost certainly Android TV or Google TV.
  2. Look in Settings. Open Settings › System › About (or Device Preferences › About). If you see "Android TV OS" or "Google TV", you're good.
  3. Look at the remote. A Google Assistant or microphone button and a YouTube or Netflix button usually means Android TV or Google TV.
  4. Not sure? Just ask. Send me your TV's brand and model (it's on a sticker on the back) and I'll check before I come out. If it can't run Umbryx TV when I arrive, you pay nothing.
